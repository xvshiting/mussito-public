Common questions

You may find the answer here.

Can I download or buy it now?

We are preparing the first public release. There is no public DMG, App Store purchase page, or public TestFlight invitation yet. Check availability and the release updates.

Can the iPhone app enter text on my Mac by itself?

The current phone-audio and Mac text-input workflow requires the Mussito Mac app. Your iPhone captures audio and controls the session; recognition, speaker filtering, and text input run on your paired Mac.

Why can't my iPhone find or connect to my Mac?

Check that the connection is enabled in the Mac app and both devices allow local network access. For initial pairing, use the same local network and keep your Mac awake. If an old link no longer works, reset pairing on the Mac and scan again. Do not post the code or link publicly.

I'm connected, but no text appears on my Mac.

Check that the recognition mode is ready, Accessibility permission is granted, and you selected an input field before starting. Local mode enters text when the session ends. In input-method mode, make sure both apps select the same virtual microphone.

Why did the session stop when I switched apps?

Switching apps or manually changing the input location can stop further input to keep text out of the wrong field. Start again at the new location. Behavior may vary between local mode and different input fields.

Do I need to install the Mac audio component?

Local recognition does not need a virtual audio component. Passing phone audio or filtered audio to an installed input method requires Mussito Audio, with the same device selected in both apps. See recognition modes on Mac.

Why did speaker filtering reject a short phrase?

Speaker verification needs enough effective speech. Very short or unclear sounds may be rejected, and overlapping speakers cannot be separated. Check the microphone, enroll your voice again, or adjust the filtering strictness.

Are Intel Macs, background recording, or a locked phone supported?

Initial hardware validation targets Apple Silicon Macs; Intel compatibility has not been verified on real hardware. For now, keep Mussito in the foreground on your iPhone and your Mac awake. Continuous recording in the background or with the phone locked is not promised.

How do I uninstall Mussito or remove its data?

Stop recording and disconnect before removing the app. Delete voice-profile data in Mac Settings and reset pairing if needed. If you installed Mussito Audio, use the app's uninstall entry and follow the restart instructions to unload the running component.

What helps us understand an issue?

Your system version, device type, Mussito version, steps to reproduce, and what you expected. Review screenshots for private information before attaching them.

Support currently uses public GitHub issues, with no guaranteed response time. For privacy questions, use the privacy question form to describe only the type of concern.
