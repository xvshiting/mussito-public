Privacy policy

Where your
voice goes.

Updated October 16, 2026. Applies to the Mussito iPhone app, Mac app, and this website.

Mussito is maintained by an independent developer and connects your iPhone with your own Mac for voice input. You can contact the maintainer through the GitHub link at the end of this policy. This policy will be updated as the public release evolves.

1. Your iPhone and your Mac

After you grant microphone access and start a session, your iPhone captures audio and sends it through an encrypted connection to your paired Mac. Initial pairing uses a QR code or pairing link. Local network access is used to discover and connect devices. The iPhone app does not directly call the recognition software or services selected on your Mac.

During ordinary voice input, audio is used only for the current session. Mussito does not save it as a recording file or write audio or transcription content to normal runtime logs. Mussito does not operate a cloud recognition server that receives or stores your voice.

2. Processing and text input on Mac

Your Mac processes the audio according to your settings, including speech detection, optional speaker filtering, and recognition. Local recognition uses a model running on your Mac. Preparing the model requires a connection to a model download provider, which may process connection information under its own policies.

If you select another installed input method or recognition tool on your Mac, admitted audio is passed to that software. Whether it connects to the internet and how it handles data depend on your selection, its settings, and its privacy policy. Review that software's information before using it.

Recognized text is entered into the Mac app you select. Once text is in that app, its storage, synchronization, and sharing depend on that app and your settings. Local-mode text input may briefly use the system clipboard. Mussito attempts to restore the previous clipboard contents if another action has not changed them.

3. Pairing and device information

Pairing credentials are stored in the system Keychain to authenticate your devices. Connection addresses, service identifiers, device names, and preferences are stored locally. Pairing links contain access credentials and should not be shared publicly. You can reset pairing in Mac Settings to invalidate old credentials.

4. Optional speaker filtering

When you enroll a voice profile, the enrollment audio is used to create a voice vector on your Mac. The vector stays on that Mac. Mussito does not upload voice-profile data to a developer server or retain an enrollment recording file. You can delete or replace the profile in Mac Settings.

5. Permissions, choices, and deletion

You can revoke microphone, local network, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring permissions in iPhone or Mac system settings. Related features may then be unavailable. Stopping or canceling a session stops further processing. Text already entered into another app must be deleted in that app.

Removing the app may not automatically remove local Mac data, Keychain credentials, or the installed audio component. Before removal, delete your voice profile, reset pairing, and follow the audio component's uninstall instructions as needed. Contact the maintainer through the support link if you have questions about local data.

6. This website and public feedback

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and includes no advertising, visitor analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process network and log information under its privacy statement.

When you submit feedback on GitHub, GitHub handles your account and feedback information under its policies. Public issues can be viewed by anyone. Do not attach pairing codes, keys, private recordings, or unredacted private text. Mussito does not automatically upload diagnostic reports. You choose whether to submit a description or screenshot after reviewing it.

7. Purchases and updates

Once the iPhone app is available on the App Store, Apple will handle purchases. The developer does not receive your card or other payment details. If the released app adds purchase verification or changes its data handling, this policy will be updated.

8. Contact and policy changes

Contact the maintainer using the GitHub privacy question form. This is a public channel: describe the type of issue first and do not submit sensitive information. Changes to this policy will update the date above. New data handling will be explained when the relevant feature is offered.
