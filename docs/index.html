A little closer to your voice.

Use your iPhone as a microphone for your Mac, or choose a built-in, headset, or external microphone on the Mac itself.

02 / WRITE

Stay where you are writing.

Select an input field on your Mac before you speak. Your Mac handles recognition and text input.

03 / YOUR VOICE

Focus on the person speaking.

Optional speaker filtering uses a voice profile on your Mac. It cannot separate people speaking at the same time.

From pairing to speaking

Pair first. Then make it natural.

Read the setup guide ↗
  1. 1

    Get your Mac ready.

    Install Mussito, grant permissions, and choose a recognition mode.

  2. 2

    Pair with your iPhone.

    Scan the code on your Mac. For first-time setup, use the same local network.

  3. 3

    Choose a field. Speak.

    Keep Mussito open on your iPhone and your Mac awake. Finish the session to complete text input.

Between your own devices

Your voice goes to your Mac.

Your iPhone sends encrypted audio to your paired Mac. You choose the recognition mode on the Mac, where your voice profile is also stored.
