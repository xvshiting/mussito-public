Getting started

Connect once.
Speak naturally.

These steps describe the current development build. They will guide first-time setup when the public Mac installer and iPhone app are available.

01 Install and allow02 Recognition on Mac03 Pair your iPhone04 Start speaking05 Your voice

01 / MAC

Install and grant permissions.

  1. Once the public release is available, get the DMG from the download page, move Mussito to Applications, and open it.
  2. Allow local network access when prompted so your Mac can discover and connect to your iPhone.
  3. Follow the app's prompts to grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring access in System Settings → Privacy & Security. These support text input, shortcuts, and protection against typing in the wrong place.
  4. Allow microphone access if you use a Mac microphone or enroll your voice on the Mac.

For first-time setup, try recording and entering text from the Mac before connecting your phone.

02 / RECOGNITION

Choose how your Mac creates text.

Local recognition

Prepare the local model in Settings. The first download is approximately 612 MB. After setup, recognition runs on your Mac and the session's text is inserted when you finish speaking. This mode does not require a virtual audio component.

Using an installed Doubao input method

Install and enable the Doubao input method on your Mac. Select that mode in Mussito Settings, install the Mussito Audio component, and select the same “Mussito Audio” device in both Mussito and the input method. Installing the component requires administrator authorization. If the device does not appear, follow the installer instructions to restart your Mac, then refresh the device list.

In this mode, the input method writes directly into the selected field in real time. Online or offline recognition is controlled by the input method's own settings. Mussito is an independent app and is not affiliated with Doubao.

For the original recording source on your Mac, choose a built-in, headset, or external microphone. Do not also use Mussito Audio as the original recording source.

03 / IPHONE

Scan the code on your own Mac.

  1. Connect your iPhone and Mac to the same local network for first-time pairing.
  2. Enable the connection in the Mac app's connection settings to display a pairing QR code.
  3. Once the iPhone app is released, install Mussito. Scan the QR code with the system camera to open Mussito and pair, or paste the pairing link in the app.
  4. Allow microphone and local network access on your iPhone. Start when the connection is ready.

Pairing codes and links contain access credentials. Use them only between your own devices, and do not post them in feedback or public screenshots. To pair again, use “Reset pairing” on your Mac.

04 / SPEAK

Select an input field first.

  1. On your Mac, click where you want the text to go in the target app.
  2. Start speaking from your iPhone. With a Mac microphone, use the default right Option key or your configured shortcut.
  3. Choose “Finish and enter.” Local mode inserts the session's result; input-method mode completes the real-time input already in progress.

Keep Mussito in the foreground on your iPhone and your Mac awake. One foreground phone recording session can last up to 10 minutes. Changing the input location may stop the session to prevent text from going to the wrong place.

Canceling stops further processing. Text already committed by the input method remains in the target app.

05 / OPTIONAL

Let your Mac recognize your voice.

Enroll a voice profile in Mac Settings, or choose “Enroll my voice with this iPhone” in the phone's connection settings. Read naturally on your own for about 12 seconds of effective speech, then enable filtering. The profile stays on your Mac and can be replaced or deleted in Settings.

Speaker filtering needs enough speech to make a decision. It may reject very short sounds and cannot separate people talking over each other. If it is not working well, check the microphone and environment before adjusting the strictness.
